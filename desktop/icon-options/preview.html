i-Buk · icon directions

Four ways to remember the app.

Each direction is built to stay recognizable at desktop, taskbar, and small shortcut sizes. Choose the metaphor that feels most like i-Buk.

01

Open book + spark

Study becomes insight.

The clearest learning signal; the spark adds a small moment of discovery.

02

Folded-page “B”

The name becomes the mark.

Most ownable as a brand symbol; it takes one extra glance to read as a book.

03

Book + planner grid

Learning meets the week.

Most specific to the product’s study-planning promise; denser at tiny sizes.

04

Bookmark shield

Progress feels protected.

Strong, trustworthy silhouette; less literal about books and more about keeping knowledge.

These are concept assets only. The Windows installer icon remains unchanged until one direction is selected.
